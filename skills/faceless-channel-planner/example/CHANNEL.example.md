# CHANNEL.md
## Channel
name: [example] small questions about space (the Hebrew channel name is given in the PDF text above this box)
niche: science and space, educational but entertaining, faceless
language: Hebrew (voiceover and titles)
audience: curious people 14+, no background needed
videos per week: 1
target length: about 2.5 minutes for the first videos
what the viewer gets: one clear, true answer to one question they always wondered about

## 1. Script rules
- script type: explainer; the Script craft rules of the faceless-channel-planner skill apply (hook, shape, sentence rules, anti-slop, output format)
- research: every research request starts with "original research, real insights, no template"; 5-8 real sources per video, mostly space agencies and universities; every claim has a link; no source = cut; every script ends with a FACT CHECK list
- format study: before the first video of a type, study why the top videos of the niche work (hooks, structure, repeating patterns), never copy
- topic rules: a knowledge gap, a counterintuitive premise, or a complete-set promise; evergreen
- tone: curious and simple, short sentences, no hype words
- length: about 370 spoken words = about 2.5 minutes (a short video, so the number of segments is small)
- each video has its own angle; never refill one template
- forbidden: copying creators, shock-only titles, fake urgency, claims without a source, real people's faces or voices

## 2. Creation settings
- look: illustrated explainer
- prompt engine: higgsfield-faceless-video (or higgsfield-video-explainer if the first is not installed; without them, written prompts to paste)
- visual style sentence (the SAME sentence inside every prompt): "Clean 2D vector educational animation, deep navy background, warm white and soft teal accents, simple geometric shapes, subtle film grain, no text, no letters, no human faces."
- clip length: 10 seconds; clips = minutes x 6 (15 clips for 2.5 minutes)
- aspect ratio: 16:9
- narrator: Hebrew, calm and friendly, medium pace (voice chosen after listening to a sample)
- music: soft ambient, always quieter than the voice
- tools: Higgsfield (model names read live, not from memory)
- credits: show the cost and get approval before every batch

## 3. Packaging rules
- thumbnails: 3 concepts per video for A/B testing, each from a different framework (before/after, mid-action moment, recognizable logos, 3-step progression, a real frame from the video); one focal subject, 2-4 words at most that do not repeat the title, strong contrast, true to the video
- titles: 3 honest options (the realization moment or the ironic gap, 60 characters at most), no promise the video does not keep
- description: 2-3 lines, then the sources list, then the AI note
- tags: a short list
- AI use question in YouTube Studio: answered per YouTube's current rules
- posting rhythm: one video a week, same day
